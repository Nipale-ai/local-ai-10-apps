Why Hearth

Everything local. Nothing watched.

Your conversations, files and habits are the most personal data you own. Hearth keeps all of it where it belongs — in your home.

Private by design

Models, prompts and answers never touch a server. There is no account, no telemetry and no way to look in — because there is nothing to look at.

Fast where it matters

The Ember H1 chip sustains 45 TOPS, so a 27-billion-parameter model answers in well under a second — no spinner, no queue, no cloud round-trip.

Whole-home reach

Wi-Fi 7, Thread and Matter built in. Talk to it from the kitchen, the office or the garden — it bridges to the devices you already own.

Works offline

Internet goes, Hearth doesn't. Every model runs on the box itself, so the assistant, the summaries and the automations keep working through any outage.

Whisper-quiet

A passive-friendly thermal design idles at 19 decibels — quieter than a library. You will notice it only when you want to.

Updates that respect you

Model updates download and verify on the box. Your data is never used for training, and you can pin any model version you like.

Questions

Asked, honestly answered.

No. Inference, memory and storage all live on the box. The only network traffic Hearth ever makes is for model updates, and those are signed, verified and optional — you can set the box to fully offline and everything keeps working.

Out of the box, Hearth ships with a tuned 27B model for conversation, a 7B model for fast tasks, and a vision model for photos. Any open model that fits in memory can be added from the app — the box handles quantization automatically.

Nothing, as far as you can tell. The assistant, file search, summaries and home automations all run locally. Cloud-only conveniences like web search pause until you reconnect — the box tells you plainly when that happens.

Hearth idles at 19 decibels, which is below the noise floor of most rooms, and draws 11 W at rest. Under sustained heavy load it rises to 24 dB and 45 W — you will feel a gentle warmth on the top, never a fan.

Through Matter and Thread, both built in. Lamps, thermostats, locks and sensors pair from the app and are controlled locally — so "lights off" still works at 2 a.m. with no cloud in the loop. Plain Wi-Fi devices work too.

The full price is the box. The companion app, the assistant, model updates and a two-year hardware warranty are all included, with no monthly fee. When a better model comes out, you download it — that is the whole business model.
