Logits

For every word in its vocabulary the model outputs a raw number called a logit — a score, not a percentage. Here are the six highest for the current step.

Softmax

Softmax turns scores into probabilities: p = es/T ÷ Σ es/T. The temperature T divides every score first. Small T sharpens the peak, large T flattens the bars.

Top-k & sampling

In practice, models keep only the top k words, renormalize, and sample one from the distribution — that is the pick you see fly into the sentence.
