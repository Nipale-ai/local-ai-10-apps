Why Hearth

Serious intelligence.
Zero exodus.

Everything a home assistant should be — fast, capable, always on — and nothing it shouldn't be. No accounts, no telemetry, no uploads.

Private by design

Inference happens on the silicon in your house. Hearth has no cloud account to sign up for and no telemetry to switch off — because there is none to switch on.

Whisper quiet

A fanless aluminum monolith with a vapor chamber. Under full load it measures below a library's ambient hum — 18 dB(A) idle, and it never spins up a fan, because there isn't one.

Answers in a breath

The Ember NPU streams a 27-billion-parameter model at up to 66 tokens per second. First token lands in well under half a second, so conversations never wait on a data center.

Works when the internet doesn't

Models, documents and automations live on local NVMe. Pull the ethernet cable, drop the Wi-Fi — the kitchen still gets its answers. Updates arrive when you're back online.

One box, whole household

Per-member profiles with their own memory, voiceprints and permissions. Six people, six assistants, one hearth — each one kept strictly to its own business.

Grows with you

The Ember model library adds new open models monthly — chat, vision, transcription, embeddings — verified and signed, installed with one tap, still fully offline after.
