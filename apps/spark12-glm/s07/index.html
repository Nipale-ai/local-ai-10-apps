Each candidate has a raw score s (its logit). Temperature T divides the scores before the softmax: cold T stretches the gaps, hot T squeezes them. A real model scores ~50,000 tokens at once and keeps the top-k — here k = 6. All scores are hand-made; no real model runs in this page.
