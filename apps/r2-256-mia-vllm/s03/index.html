Why Hearth

Everything you use AI for.
Staying home.

One box serves every screen in the house — phones, laptops, TVs and speakers — with models that run on its own silicon. No subscriptions, no data trail.

Runs fully on-device

Language, speech and vision models execute on Hearth's 60-TOPS neural engine. Unplug the internet and everything still works.

Whisper quiet

Fanless, sealed aluminium shell. At 11 dB(A) you will hear the page turn, not the computer.

Every screen in the house

Phones, laptops and TVs on your network share one Hearth. Private by profile, so everyone's context stays their own.

Private by construction

No accounts, no analytics, no outbound calls. The network tab stays empty — and there's a physical kill switch to prove it.

Knows your world

Point Hearth at folders, notes or recipes and it builds a private index on disk. Answers come with sources you can open.

Fits your stack

An OpenAI-compatible local API, Matter and Thread support, and apps for iOS, Android, macOS and Windows.
