How a language model writes, one word at a time

It doesn't plan the whole sentence. It looks at the words so far, gives every possible next word a probability, picks one — and repeats.

Low T divides the logits by a small number, so the largest score wins almost everything — careful, predictable text. High T flattens the scores, giving weaker candidates a real chance — more creative, more risky.
