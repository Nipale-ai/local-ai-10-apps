Why Hearth?

Intelligence shouldn't cost your privacy.

100% Local Processing

Every model runs on Hearth's custom silicon. Your voice data never touches the internet. It stays in the room, in your house, under your control.

Privacy by Design

No microphones are active unless you tap Hearth's top. A physical mute switch kills the audio circuit. The LED ring goes dark. You decide when AI listens.

Always Ready, Zero Latency

Because Hearth processes locally, responses are instant. No waiting for a cloud round-trip. Commands execute in under 200 milliseconds, every time.

Expandable Intelligence

Install new capabilities over time. Hearth supports plugins for music, smart home, coding assistants, and more. Your device grows with your needs.

Silently Beautiful

Zero fans. Zero noise. Hearth uses a solid-state heatsink and passive cooling. It's the quietest device on your desk — and the smartest.

Works With Everything

Connect Hearth to your lights, thermostat, speakers, or security system. It speaks Matter, Zigbee, and Thread. One device replaces five hubs.
