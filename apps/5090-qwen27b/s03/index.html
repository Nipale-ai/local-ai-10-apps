Why Hearth

Everything a home AI should be.
Nothing it shouldn't.

Private by design

Your voice, photos and documents never leave the house. There is no cloud account, no telemetry and no ads — the hardware has no way to send your data out, because it isn't built to.

Works without internet

Unplug the router and Hearth keeps answering, summarising and organising. Every model runs on the box itself, not in a data centre you can't see.

Fast enough to feel instant

A 40 TOPS neural engine answers everyday questions in under a second. No round-trip to a server, no spinner, no waiting for your words to come back.

Whisper quiet

At 18 decibels, Hearth is quieter than a library and about as loud as the fridge in the next room. Set it on a bookshelf and forget it's there.

One cable, that's all

Plug it in beside your router and it's ready in about four minutes. No apps to install, no setup wizard, no account to create.

Grows with you

New models arrive as small, signed downloads — and you decide which to keep. Your Hearth, your rules, your house.
