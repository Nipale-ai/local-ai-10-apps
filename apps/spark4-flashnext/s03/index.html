Why Hearth

Private by design. Silent by engineering.

Four reasons people replace the smart speaker with a hearth.

Nothing leaves the house

Every model runs on the silicon inside Hearth. There is no cloud service to send your data to — the network radio can even be switched off entirely.

Instant answers

No round trip to a data center. Hearth replies in under 200 milliseconds, whether the internet is up, down, or congested.

Whisper quiet

Passive cooling, no fans, no coil whine. At full load Hearth measures under 18 dB — quieter than the room tone of a library.

Yours, not rented

Buy it once. Models are updated over the local network, features never expire, and there is no subscription between you and your own home.
