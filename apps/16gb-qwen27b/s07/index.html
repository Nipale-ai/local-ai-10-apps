p(w) = es(w) / T  ÷  Σv es(v) / T

Each candidate word w carries a raw score s(w) — engineers call it a logit. Dividing by the temperature T and applying the softmax turns those scores into probabilities that add up to exactly 1. Low T sharpens the odds toward the favorite; high T flattens them and gives wilder words a real shot.

Real models usually trim the list first — keeping only the top k candidates (top-k) or cutting where the cumulative probability reaches about 0.9 (top-p). We keep just five so you can see every bar.
